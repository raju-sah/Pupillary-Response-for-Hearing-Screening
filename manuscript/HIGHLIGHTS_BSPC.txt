Highlights:
• End-to-end deep learning framework for objective optical hearing screening.
• Standardized 21,164-trial leak-free benchmark across three clinical cohorts.
• CNN-Transformer attains SOTA 0.844 ROC-AUC and 4.2x PR-AUC gain over baseline.
• Self-attention isolates 0.8-2.2s LC-NE dilation window without supervision.
• Truncating trials to 1.5s retains ~96% accuracy, halving test duration.
